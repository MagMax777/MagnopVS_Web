## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes classical Japanese yokai lore and shonen manga dynamism with Tokyo 3000 high-tech urban decay. Designed for cinematic narrative interfaces, companion applications, and occult visual novels, it targets players and readers immersed in dark urban fantasy and futuristic mythologies.

The interface evokes hyper-stylized mysticism: tactile digital talismans (*ofuda*), neon-infused ink wash textures (*sumi-e*), and tactical HUD overlays. The overarching aesthetic fuses **Brutalism** and **Tactile Cyberpunk**, featuring angled panel divisions reminiscent of manga layouts, razor-sharp technical borders, deep matte backgrounds, and luminous spiritual accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an obsidian stage illuminated by bioluminescent spirit energy and tactical warning indicators.

- **Primary (`#75F0D3` - Spirit Aquamarine):** Serves as the primary luminescent spirit-energy anchor. Used for critical interactive states, activated seals, key game inputs, and radiant occult highlights.
- **Secondary (`#62B8E8` - Celestial Blue):** Represents cold cybernetic HUD accents, secondary indicators, scanlines, and diagnostic data points.
- **Tertiary (`#7957C8` - Ethereal Violet):** Channels mystical void energy, ritual circles, specialized spell/status effects, and deeper ambient backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#090A0F` - Matte Void Black):** The base background surface, absorbing extraneous glare to maximize the potency of glowing glyphs.
- **Surface Variant (`#151722` - Deep Surface Slate):** Used for manga panels, containment modules, and cards.
- **Accent High-Alert (`#E63946` / `#D34DAA` - Exorcism Crimson / Cursed Magenta):** Reserved strictly for lethal warnings, high-consequence decisions, cursed states, and health critical indicators.
- **Foreground Base (`#F1EEE8` - Pearl White):** High-legibility text, primary dialogue labels, and stark character portraits.
- **Muted Text (`#777B89` - Ash Grey):** Structural metadata, inactive tech tiers, and tactical grid lines.

## Typography

The typography unites mechanical futuristic precision with narrative readability.

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Chosen for its engineered, tech-brutalist presence that mirrors sharp katana geometry and futuristic manga titles.
- **Narrative & Body (`Geist`):** Delivers clean neutral legibility across long-form visual novel dialogues, tactical log records, and lore manifests without eye fatigue.
- **HUD & Glyph Indicators (`Space Mono`):** Dedicated to technical readouts, ritual hex coordinates, talisman indices, cooldown timers, and system feedback.
- **Kanji Integration:** When kanji or seal glyphs accompany headers, render them with a matching optical baseline and a subtle glow, styled as ancient script modernized into vector glyphs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in dynamic manga framing (*koma*) translated into a responsive 12-column digital grid (4-column on mobile, 8-column on tablet).

- **Asymmetric Narrative Panels:** Surface containers break standard symmetrical borders. Visual novel viewports and HUD frames can utilize diagonal edge-cuts (chamfered corners) or split gutter ratios to evoke graphic novel layouts.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Built on a disciplined 4px base increment. Touch targets honor a minimum interactive footprint of 48px to preserve responsiveness during mobile gameplay.
- **Mobile First Touch Bounds:** Controls, dialogue progression triggers, and ritual triggers anchor to the lower 35% of the viewport (the thumb arc), ensuring fluid one-handed operation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons diffuse organic drop-shadows in favor of **Tonal Stratification, High-Tech Ghost Borders, and Bioluminescent Radiance**.

- **Ground Plane (`#090A0F`):** Deep matte black void; base canvas.
- **Tier 1 - Structural Containment (`#151722`):** Panel surfaces framed by 1px solid low-opacity borders (`rgba(119, 123, 137, 0.2)`).
- **Tier 2 - Interactive Nodes & Data Frames:** Elevated slates marked by sharp inner borders and chamfered accent brackets.
- **Tier 3 - Overlays & Modal Exorcisms:** Translucent slate backed by backdrop blur, bordered by `#62B8E8` or `#7957C8` hairline contours.
- **Luminescence & Glow:** Depth for critical or mystical elements uses tight, high-intensity neon glows:
  - *Spirit Glow:* `0 0 12px rgba(117, 240, 211, 0.45), 0 0 2px #75F0D3`
  - *Cursed Blood Glow:* `0 0 12px rgba(230, 57, 70, 0.5), 0 0 2px #E63946`

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**, reflecting cold cybernetic interfaces and the slash of a blade.

- **Chamfers & Angles:** Replace traditional border radii with 45-degree chamfers (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) on card corners, buttons, and HUD dialog nodes.
- **Ofuda Ratios:** Vertical talisman cards maintain a 1:2.4 slender aspect ratio with sharp edges and decorative header brackets.
- **Geometric Trims:** Borders utilize broken hairline intersections, technical corner crosshairs (`+`), and sacred geometry motifs (circles inscribed in octagons) to frame focal elements.

## Components

### Buttons & Talisman Triggers
- **Primary Action (Spirit Blade):** High-contrast background filled with `#75F0D3`, foreground typography in `#090A0F`, sharp edges with opposite chamfered corners. On hover/active, emits an aquamarine aura and shifts text to bold.
- **Secondary Action (HUD Outline):** Deep surface `#151722` with a 1px `#62B8E8` outline and `#62B8E8` monospace text. Corner accents feature small 2x2px tech pips.
- **Destructive/Cursed Action:** Deep `#090A0F` background, framed by `#E63946`, emitting a low crimson pulse on active states.

### Cards & Manga Panels
- Encased in `#151722` with top-right or bottom-left diagonal corner cutouts.
- Visual novel panels use stylized black ink-wash bleed overlays along lower margins.
- Top borders feature tiny technical metadata tags (`ID // 3000-OCCULT-NODE`) styled in `label-sm`.

### Input Fields
- Underline-only or structural 3-sided border in `#777B89`.
- When focused, transitions to sharp `#75F0D3` edge with an active spirit-glow border and an adjacent Japanese seal indicator glyph.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Seals
- **Checkboxes:** Square technical reticles (0px radius) that display a glowing ritual rune (`#75F0D3`) when checked.
- **Radios:** Diamond-oriented frames (`transform: rotate(45deg)`) holding an inner glowing core when selected.

### Chips & Tactical Status Badges
- Ultra-compact, all-caps monospace labels (`label-sm`).
- Enclosed in a 1px border corresponding to their elemental energy type (Celestial Blue, Spirit Aquamarine, Ethereal Violet, or Crimson) with a 10% alpha fill of the same hue.

### Ritual Wheel / Tactical Dial
- Circular HUD dial overlaying geometric summoning matrices. Displays rotational kanji coordinates, cooling down with radial spirit-glow fills.
