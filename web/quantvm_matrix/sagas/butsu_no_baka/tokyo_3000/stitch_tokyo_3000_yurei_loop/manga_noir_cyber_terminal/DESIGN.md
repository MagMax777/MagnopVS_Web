## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-contrast aesthetic fusion of Japanese Futurism, Cyberpunk, Neo-Gothic architecture, and Manga Noir paneling. Designed for tactical narrative environments and diegetic combat terminals, the visual tone is stark, severe, and mechanical.

### Visual Character
- **Ink & Phosphor Aesthetic:** Compositions mimic dense black-ink serialization pages backlit by a monochromatic display terminal. Solid masses of deep black collide directly with paper-white structural accents and critical blood-red focal triggers.
- **Architectural Edge:** Form factors are hyper-geometric, sharp-angled, and clinical. Elements reject organic curves and soft bubbles in favor of chamfered corners, hairline borders, and tactical data density.
- **Manga Paneling Logic:** Layout structures borrow from sequential art: asymmetrical framing, hard gutter gutters, heavy framing borders, and rhythmic diagonal cutaways.
- **Atmospheric Restraint:** Dynamic tension is maintained strictly through extreme luminance contrast and precise, surgical placement of red alerts.

## Colors

The design system enforces an absolute, non-negotiable monochromatic foundation anchored by an aggressive crimson focal value. Chromatism outside this gamut is strictly forbidden: no cyan, blue, green, amber, orange, or violet under any circumstance.

### Distribution Hierarchy
- **65% Obsidian / Pure Black (`#050505`, `#0B0B0D`):** Canvas voids, shadow depths, structural panels, and base underlays.
- **20% Stark White / Silver (`#FFFFFF`, `#E6E6E8`):** Dominant typography, high-priority icons, primary buttons, and critical framing geometry.
- **10% Steel / Graphite (`#19191D`, `#29292E`, `#858585`, `#A0A0A5`):** Structural borders, grid crosshairs, secondary technical readouts, deactivated inputs, and surface tiering.
- **5% Blood Red (`#C8102E`):** Surgical accents, lethal state changes, danger flags, targeted reticles, and energetic neon hairlines.

### Functional Surfaces
- **Canvas (`#050505`):** The absolute abyss base.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#0B0B0D`):** Base structural compartments and primary panels.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#141417`):** Elevated modules, nested tactical screens, and hover overlays.
- **Border Default (`#29292E`):** Baseline 1px mechanical containment lines.
- **Border Active (`#C8102E`):** Target-lock, alert, and active selection state.

## Typography

Typography acts as both raw graphic instrumentation and narrative delivery. The typographic hierarchy utilizes high-velocity geometric grotesques for display and classification tasks, paired with an ultra-neutral, legible grotesque for dense information readouts.

### Usage Directives
- **Case Treatment:** All labels, data tags, metrics, and primary buttons must be rendered in full uppercase with expanded letter spacing (`0.1em` to `0.16em`).
- **Tactical Micro-Typography:** Sub-labels (`label-sm`) serve as technical serials, panel indices (e.g., `SEC_04 // ALPHA`), and timestamp readouts.
- **Headline Aggression:** Large headings (`display-lg`, `display-md`) demand tight tracking and heavy weight, evoking manga sound effects and industrial facility signage.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a manga panel structural system executed via a rigid, non-fluid 12-column grid. Panels lock against each other with disciplined precision, creating high-tension structural seams rather than soft ambient whitespace.

### Breakpoint Matrix
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns. Gutter: `1rem`, Margins: `1rem`. Panels stack vertically into full-width sequential frames with 1px border intersections.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns. Gutter: `1rem`, Margins: `2rem`. Two-tier hierarchical layouts with persistent technical side-rails.
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns. Gutter: `1.5rem`, Margins: `3rem`. Max content bound at `1600px`. Complex multi-panel manga grids with asymmetrical data sidebars and command viewports.

### Spacing Constraints
- Element gaps inside cards and modules should strictly rely on compact units (`space-xs` to `space-md`) to emulate condensed avionics HUDs and cybernetic terminals.
- Macro spacing between distinct structural sectors utilizes `space-xl` to mirror the deliberate visual pauses between manga page sequences.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects soft drop shadows and diffuse organic light sources. Depth is achieved via hard surfaces, border contrasts, and tactical neon glows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Ground 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure obsidian `#050505` with an optional 1px repeating horizontal scanline pattern (opacity: 3%).
- **Tier 1 (Framed Containers):** Background `#0B0B0D` encased in a crisp 1px `#29292E` outline. No drop shadow.
- **Tier 2 (Popovers / Floating Overlays):** Background `#141417`, bordered in 1px `#858585`. Projected depth is conveyed exclusively via a hard, non-blurred offset shadow: `4px 4px 0px #000000`.
- **Tier 3 (Active Focus / Lethal Alert):** Border shifts to 1px `#C8102E` with a localized razor-thin red neon edge bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 8px rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The geometry of this system is intentionally stark and severe. Roundness value is set to `0` (absolute sharp corners).

### Architectural Rules
- **Corner Treatments:** Every container, input field, chip, and button features sharp 90-degree corners.
- **Angular Cutouts (Chamfer Metaphor):** Where mechanical differentiation is required, panels and high-priority action buttons employ 45-degree corner clips (`clip-path: polygon(...)` 6px to 10px cut) on the top-right and bottom-left diagonal axis.
- **Dividers & Seams:** Dividers must always be rendered as crisp 1px rules in `#29292E`, broken periodically by 3px square decorative registration marks in `#858585`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (High Execution):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with `#050505` text in `Space Grotesk Bold` uppercase. Sharp corners or 8px top-right chamfer. On hover, background shifts instantly to `#C8102E` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Secondary (Terminal Action):** Ghost style. Transparent background, 1px `#858585` border, `#FFFFFF` text. On hover, border and text snap to `#FFFFFF` with an inner 10% white overlay.
- **Destructive / Supernatural Focus:** Solid `#C8102E` fill, `#FFFFFF` text. On hover, intense pulse bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.7)`).
- **Disabled:** Transparent fill, 1px dashed `#29292E` border, `#858585` text.

### Chips & Tactical Badges
- Ultra-compact, sharp rectangular containers (`padding: 2px 6px`).
- Default: Border 1px `#29292E`, background `#0B0B0D`, text `#A0A0A5` in `label-sm`.
- Critical/Active: Border 1px `#C8102E`, background `rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.1)`, text `#FFFFFF` prefixed with a filled square red pip (`■`).

### Input Fields
- Flat rectangular field, background `#0B0B0D`, bottom-only or full 1px border in `#29292E`.
- Typography: `Inter`, 14px, `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder text in `#858585`.
- Focus State: Border transitions instantly to `#C8102E`. Left vertical indicator tab appears (2px solid `#C8102E`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkbox: Sharp 14x14px square, 1px border in `#858585`, background `#050505`. Checked state: Solid `#C8102E` fill containing a pure white micro-tick or center cross (`+`).
- Radio: Diamond-oriented 12x12px square (rotated 45deg) with 1px border in `#858585`. Selected state: Solid `#FFFFFF` diamond core.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed to resemble sequential manga panels and combat diagnostic monitors.
- Background: `#0B0B0D`. Border: 1px `#29292E`.
- Header Area: Separated by a 1px solid rule, featuring technical panel designations (e.g., `// PANEL_SYS.01`) in `label-sm` tracking `#858585`.
- Corner Accents: Optional 4-corner L-bracket crosshairs in `#858585` to enforce targeting/terminal themes.

### Specialized Elements
- **HUD Reticles:** Hairline circular and square alignment markers layered over data visualizers.
- **Barcode / Hex Stamp:** Metadata serial numbers placed along the outer margins of cards to ground the diegetic reality of Neo-Tokyo.
