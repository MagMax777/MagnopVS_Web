# Arise-Inspired Modules for MagnopVS Life Hero

These modules translate the "Soft-Tech" and "Biologically Responsive" philosophy of the Arise (2026) ecosystem into the high-fidelity Cosmic HUD of MagnopVS.

## 1. Módulo de Descompresión Cognitiva (Meditation)
*   **Philosophy:** Minimalist interface that "breathes" with the user.
*   **Feature:** Background transitions and glow intensity tied to HRV (Heart Rate Variability) data.
*   **UI:** Buttonless layout, gesture-driven, haptic-focused.

## 2. Jardín Digital de Hábitos (Habit Tracker)
*   **Philosophy:** Organic visualization of discipline.
*   **Feature:** Habits grow as "Aether Flora" based on consistency. High glassmorphism and interactive bioluminescent widgets.
*   **UI:** Bento-box style with 3D elements.

## 3. Entrenamiento Biométrico de Alta Velocidad (Fitness)
*   **Philosophy:** legible from a distance, high contrast, utility-first.
*   **Feature:** 3D muscle heatmaps and "Ghost Avatar" training partners projected in the environment.
*   **UI:** Adaptive complexity—strips away data during high-stress sets to prevent cognitive overload.
