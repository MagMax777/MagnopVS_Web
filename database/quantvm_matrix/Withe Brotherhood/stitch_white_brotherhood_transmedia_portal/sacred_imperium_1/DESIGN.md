## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a divine authoritarian intelligence doctrine: power presented as absolute cosmic order. It merges classical aristocratic heraldry and occult sacred geometry with cold, hyper-advanced military surveillance. The experience is designed to evoke awe, ritualistic submission, severe elegance, and absolute institutional certainty.

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily from High-Contrast Minimalist Brutalism and Sacred Technical Modernism. Interfaces must feel like consecrated digital relics or classified dossiers housed within black-granite subterranean command vaults. Hairline structural grids, ceremonial golden ratios, precise telemetry data, and monolithic stark ivory architectural panels establish an unyielding hierarchy.

Every interaction conveys ceremonial weight: deliberate, sharp, and absolute. There are no playful animations or soft organic shapes; motion is crisp, optical, and decisive, echoing classified cryptographic decryptions and sovereign decrees.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritarian ceremonial hierarchy through strict contrast ratios:

- **Void & Obsidian (`#050505`, `#0D0D0D`, `#141414`)**: Forms the infinite background void and foundational surface containers. Surfaces utilize subtle shifts in obsidian luminance to delineate depth rather than diffuse drop shadows.
- **Architectural White & Ivory (`#F4F1E8`, `#FFFFFF`)**: Represents unyielding monolithic authority. Used for high-impact structural dossier slabs, primary imperial headlines, and uncompromised structural grid lines.
- **Sacred Gold (`#C9A227`, `#E6C45A`)**: Denotes sovereignty, sacred technology, high-command clearance, and divine seals. Applied to precision hairline rules, cryptographic sigils, and primary interactive perimeters.
- **Signal & Surveillance Yellow (`#FFD400`, `#FFF000`)**: Reserved strictly for threat detection, telemetry tracking, classified security clearances, and redacted text reveals. It must never be diluted with casual usage.

Apply colors strictly by clearance level: void backgrounds absorb ambient space, architectural white anchors sovereign content, sacred gold accents divine instrumentation, and surveillance yellow pierces through with urgent tactical precision.

## Typography

Typography establishes an uncompromising dialogue between divine sovereign decree and classified computational intelligence.

- **Imperial Display (`Bodoni Moda`)**: High-contrast, sharp serif architecture. Set in uppercase or formal title case with generous tracking (`0.03em` to `0.08em`) to mimic Roman lapidary inscriptions and sacred codices.
- **System Telemetry & Metadata (`Space Mono`)**: Exacting, monospaced computational rigor. Utilized for cryptographic hashes, planetary coordinates, surveillance vectors, security tiers, and system status indicators. Always letter-spaced and capitalised for technical labels.
- **Doctrinal Body (`Inter`)**: Neutral, neutral-density modern grotesk providing cold legibility against deep black or ivory surfaces. Body text must remain crisp, unembellished, and optimized for dense intelligence briefings.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture is built on a 12-column rigid mathematical grid governed by sacred symmetry and military intelligence dashboards.

- **Rhythm**: Layouts employ strict axial alignment. Central axes anchor monumental headers and sacred insignia, while telemetry data flanks the perimeter in disciplined data columns.
- **Margins & Gutters**: Outer canvas margins enforce an untouchable ceremonial frame (`3rem` desktop, `1rem` mobile). Elements snap strictly to column boundaries with `1.5rem` desktop gutters and `0.75rem` mobile gutters.
- **Responsiveness**: 
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: Multi-column dossier view with simultaneous telemetry readouts, sacred centerpieces, and side-rail classified streams.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout; technical side-rails collapse into tabbed cryptographic modules.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column single-axis stack; telemetry condenses into hairline micro-bars with edge-to-edge section dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects standard soft drop shadows in favor of stark planar stratification, razor-thin structural borders, and targeted emissive radiation.

- **Obsidian Tiering**: Surfaces stack through absolute luminance steps: Void (`#050505`) to Primary Layer (`#0D0D0D`) to Elevated Consoles (`#141414`).
- **Hairline Framing**: Depth is bounded by 1px borders in Sacred Gold (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.35)` to `#C9A227`) or Architectural White (`rgba(244, 241, 232, 0.2)`).
- **Emissive Tactical Glows**: Focused directional luminescence highlights sacred items and threat monitors. Critical nodes cast a concentrated glow (`0 0 12px rgba(255, 212, 0, 0.45)` for threat alerts; `0 0 16px rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.3)` for sovereign relics).
- **Ceremonial Dossier Inversion**: Dossiers use high-contrast physical inversions: stark ivory slabs (`#F4F1E8`) cast over obsidian voids with razor-sharp black shadows (`0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.95)`).

## Shapes

The interface geometry is entirely uncompromising: `roundedness: 0` (Sharp). 

All containers, buttons, dossiers, and data fields utilize absolute 90-degree angles. Any curved shape is strictly reserved for sacred geometry overlays, planetary crosshairs, or circular optical targeting reticles rendered as hairline vectors.

Panels may incorporate 45-degree chamfered corners (clip-path notches of 4px to 8px) on security-critical dossiers and clearance badges to evoke military intelligence hardware and ceremonial stone masonry.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Imperial Authority (Primary)**: Solid Ivory (`#F4F1E8`) background, jet black text (`#050505`), Space Mono uppercase bold. 1px solid gold hover outline with zero corner radius.
- **Sacred Relic (Secondary)**: Obsidian background (`#0D0D0D`), 1px gold border (`#C9A227`), gold text (`#E6C45A`). Hover triggers an internal emissive gold gradient (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.15)`).
- **Surveillance Override (Alert)**: 1px warning yellow border (`#FFD400`), yellow text, high-frequency hover strobe.

### Dossier Cards
- Structural ivory or obsidian plaques bounded by hairline gold corner notches.
- Header fields include classified clearance classifications (`LEVEL-V DOCTRINE`), hash tags, and timestamp indexes in `label-sm`.
- Dossier content rests on textured, faint marble or scanline noise layers.

### Redacted Censor Blocks
- Pure black rectangular blocks (`#000000`) completely covering sensitive doctrinal text.
- Interaction (hover or tap) reveals the obscured intelligence in high-intensity surveillance yellow (`#FFD400`) accompanied by a subtle hairline targeting bracket.

### Input Fields & Terminal Consoles
- Flush obsidian field backgrounds with 1px border (`rgba(244, 241, 232, 0.25)`). Focus shifts the border to active Sacred Gold (`#C9A227`) with a monospaced terminal cursor.
- Field labels are displayed in all-caps `label-md` with leading section runes or numerical indexing (e.g., `// SEC-01 :: CLEARANCE_CIPHER`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp square indicators (0px radius) framed by 1px gold or white wireframes.
- Active states feature a solid center square fill or an imperial Greek cross (+) glyph in Signal Yellow (`#FFD400`).

### Telemetry Trackers & Status Badges
- Micro-scale telemetry chips featuring monospaced tracking indicators, pulse dots, and coordinate strings.
- Status values: `DIVINE_ACCORD` (Gold), `OBSERVED` (Ivory), `HERESY_DETECTED` (Signal Yellow).
