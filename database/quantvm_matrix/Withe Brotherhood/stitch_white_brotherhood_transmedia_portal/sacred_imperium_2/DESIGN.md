## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a divine authoritarian cosmic intelligence doctrine: absolute power realized through unyielding order, sacred geometry, and cold imperial surveillance. It fuses high-contrast monumental classicism with razor-sharp aerospace telemetry and occult-tech ceremonial hierarchy. The aesthetic evokes sublime awe, absolute institutional finality, and disciplined cosmic scale.

The visual style synthesizes High-Contrast Technical Brutalism, Sacred Geometry, and Monolithic Minimalist Architecture. Interfaces present as classified astronomical relics, ceremonial stone slabs, or hyper-advanced command telemetry decks housed within subterranean obsidian citadels. Structural grids, hairline golden ratios, encrypted telemetry blocks, and stark ivory monolithic cards govern the space with uncompromising authority.

All motion and interactive behaviors are deliberate, instantaneous, and definitive. Animations discard organic softening in favor of optical sweeps, sharp reticle locks, cryptographic decryptions, and sovereign coordinate snaps.

## Colors

The color system establishes a severe ritualistic hierarchy built upon extreme contrast between absolute abyssal blacks, architectural ivory stone, ceremonial auric metals, and tactical warning beacons:

- **Void Obsidian (`#050505`) & Panel Surfaces (`#0D0D0D`, `#141414`)**: Anchor the system in a deep space vacuum. Elevation is expressed via strict luminance shifts between void baselines and micro-stepped planar containers rather than diffuse atmospheric glows.
- **Sacred Ivory (`#F4F1E8`)**: Serves as the primary chromatic force representing sovereign imperial decree. It is deployed for monumental headers, inverted dossier plaques, active navigation states, and primary architectural boundaries.
- **Imperial Gold (`#C9A227`) & Royal Gold (`#E6C45A`)**: Represent divine lineage, occult technologies, high-tier clearances, and sacred coordinate systems. Applied to 1px geometric alignment rules, sigil glyphs, tactical brackets, and critical system triggers.
- **Surveillance Warning Yellow (`#FFD400`)**: Reserved strictly for high-threat classifications, orbital anomalies, sensor locks, active telemetry spikes, and unredacted intelligence reveals.

Color is strictly tied to administrative and occult clearance tiers: lower-echelon system data remains grounded in subdued ivory and obsidian, gold denotes divine command authority, and surveillance yellow indicates an urgent tactical breach.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy arbitrates between sovereign heraldic law, cold planetary telemetry, and dense institutional records:

- **Imperial Display (`Bodoni Moda`)**: High-contrast, sharp serif letterforms echoing stone lapidary inscriptions, royal charters, and classical monumentality. Headings are rendered in all-caps or formal title case with expansive tracking (`0.03em` to `0.08em`) to convey sovereign permanence.
- **Telemetry, Coordinates, & Ranks (`Space Mono`)**: Unforgiving, fixed-pitch computational typography. Deployed across planetary coordinates, resource tickers, zoom scales, threat alerts, and classified dossier stamps. Always tracked generously (`0.1em` to `0.2em`) and formatted in uppercase.
- **Institutional Lore & Citizen Data (`Inter`)**: A neutral, unembellished grotesk engineered for dense tactical readouts, diplomatic archives, and long-form narrative codices against deep obsidian backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

Spatial organization relies on a rigorous 12-column mathematical grid combined with deep space bento architecture and axial focal alignments:

- **Bento Hierarchy & Modular Panels**: The viewport divides into defined, non-overlapping tactical containers: fixed command tickers, nested scale navigators, isometric sector viewports, and streaming surveillance rails.
- **Margins & Gutters**: Outer margins preserve an untouchable ceremonial frame (`3rem` on desktop, `1rem` on mobile) to frame UI panels like illuminated planetary schematics. Gutters maintain sharp, consistent gaps (`1.5rem` desktop, `0.75rem` mobile) ensuring no structural borders collide or visually merge.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column expansive command layout with simultaneous multi-tier resource tickers, center isometric staging, and persistent lateral dossier feeds.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout; secondary telemetry modules collapse into expandable accordion dossiers and tabbed overlays.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column linear vertical stack. Tactical tickers become compact horizontal swipe rails; focal views prioritize single-tier data with bottom-anchored scale switches.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects organic soft drop shadows in favor of geometric stratification, hairline structural boundaries, and directional emissive locks:

- **Planar Obsidian Stratification**: Depth builds upward through controlled luminance tiers: `void-obsidian` (`#050505`) as the base substrate, `panel-surface` (`#0D0D0D`) for primary bento modules, and `surface-elevated` (`#141414`) for modal overlays and active telemetry monitors.
- **Hairline Structural Framing**: Layers are separated by 1px borders using Sacred Gold at 30% to 100% opacity or Architectural Ivory at 20% opacity. No blurred drop shadows are permitted on standard surfaces.
- **Ceremonial Dossier Inversion**: High-priority cards physically invert the palette, placing sharp Sacred Ivory (`#F4F1E8`) slabs directly onto Void Obsidian with hard-edged cast occlusions (`0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.95)`).
- **Emissive Tactical Targeting**: High-threat or active sovereign elements emit focused, low-radius luminescence (`0 0 12px rgba(255, 212, 0, 0.45)` for surveillance alerts; `0 0 16px rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.35)` for consecrated relics).

## Shapes

The geometric philosophy is uncompromisingly architectural: `roundedness: 0` (Sharp).

All visual containers, button surfaces, resource cards, and modal dialogs use precise 90-degree corners. Any curved geometries are exclusively reserved for sacred astronomical rings, planetary targeting reticles, orbital trajectory arcs, and concentric radar displays rendered strictly as hairline 1px paths.

Containers and clearance badges may incorporate 45-degree corner chamfers (notches of `4px` to `8px`) created via geometric clipping to reference military hardware housings and monolithic stone blocks.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Imperial Decree (Primary)**: Solid Sacred Ivory (`#F4F1E8`) fill, jet-black typography (`#050505`), `Space Mono` uppercase bold, sharp 90-degree edges. Hover shifts border to 1px solid Imperial Gold (`#C9A227`) with instantaneous color snap.
- **Relic Trigger (Secondary)**: Void Obsidian background (`#0D0D0D`), 1px Imperial Gold perimeter (`#C9A227`), Royal Gold text (`#E6C45A`). Hover initiates an internal gold emissive sheen (`rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.15)`).
- **Tactical Intercept (Warning/Alert)**: 1px Surveillance Yellow border (`#FFD400`), Surveillance Yellow text, high-frequency hover strobe effect.

### 4X Resource Tickers (Matter, Energy, Knowledge, Wealth, Influence, Agency, Astral Shards)
- Ultra-compact tabular modules positioned at screen perimeters.
- Rendered in `Space Mono` `label-md` with 1px gold vertical dividers.
- Displays asset acronym, numeric counter, and real-time delta indicators (`+14.2/cycle` in Royal Gold, `-3.1/cycle` in Surveillance Yellow).

### Nested Scale Level Navigator (Homunculus -> City -> Guild -> Region -> Planet -> Cosmos)
- Segmented linear controller utilizing 0px radius architectural segments.
- Inactive tiers display faint ivory borders with dimmed `Space Mono` text; active tier switches to solid Sacred Ivory slab inversion with bold black lettering and hairline gold brackets framing the active tier.

### Cards & Dossiers
- Monolithic bento containers structured by 1px gold or muted ivory perimeter lines.
- Dossier headers feature formal clearance tags (`CLEARANCE: TIER-IV`), sector coordinates, and Roman numeral registry indexes in `label-sm`.
- Sensitive archival entries employ solid black censor bars that reveal classified text in Surveillance Yellow (`#FFD400`) on interaction.

### Form Inputs & Terminal Fields
- Recessed obsidian fields (`#050505`) with 1px border (`rgba(244, 241, 232, 0.25)`).
- Active state transitions the border to Imperial Gold (`#C9A227`) with an active terminal cursor block.
- Labels are rendered in all-caps `label-md` with technical prefixes (e.g., `// INPUT :: SECTOR_CIPHER`).

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Toggles
- 0px sharp square indicators bounded by 1px wireframes.
- Checked state fills with an Imperial Greek Cross (`+`) glyph or solid square center in Surveillance Yellow or Sacred Gold.
- Radio buttons feature diamond rotatings (`45deg`) rather than rounded circles.

### Isometric Zoning Markers & Reticles
- 1px hairline targeting matrices overlaying sector viewports.
- Intersecting alignment crosshairs with corner angle ticks and real-time astronomical coordinate readouts in `label-sm`.
