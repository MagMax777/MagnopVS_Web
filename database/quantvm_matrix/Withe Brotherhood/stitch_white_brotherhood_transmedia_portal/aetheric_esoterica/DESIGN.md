## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, tactile, and ceremonial visual language named Aetheric Esoterica—where 19th-century Victorian apparatus, industrial precision, and arcane metallurgy collide with cyclopean eldritch horror. The narrative context balances empirical industrial engineering (clockwork gears, vacuum chambers, manometers, riveted brass housings) with the unfathomable abyss of non-Euclidean cosmic geometry.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Tactile Neo-Skeuomorphic Esotericism**:
- **Physical Apparatus:** Surfaces behave like machined, patinated matter—cold-rolled steel, tarnished brass sheeting, and heavy glass containment bezels. Interactive triggers carry mechanical weight, physical travel, and mechanical clicks.
- **Eldritch Bioluminescence & Radium Radiance:** Dark void surfaces act as deep matter sinks, pierced by the sickening phosphorescence of radium-etched calibrations and vacuum tubes carrying aetheric charges.
- **Ornate Industrial Geometry:** Precision Victorian filigree is fused with disturbing organic curve topologies—subtle tentacular motifs, Calabi-Yau dimensional folds, and alchemical distillation diagrams.
- **Target Audience & Tone:** Connoisseurs of cosmic dread, investigative scholars, transmedia occultists, and dark speculative fiction enthusiasts. The emotional experience balances scholastic dread, forbidden knowledge, and the tangible luxury of antique imperial instruments.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chemical, metallic, and ethereal properties of late-Victorian esoteric machinery exposed to otherworldly atmospheres.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#c49346` — Burnished Brass / Auric Alloy):** Represents mechanical integrity, structural filigree, ornamental borders, and primary interactive indicators. In hovered or electrified states, shifts toward hot specular gold (`#ffd276`).
- **Secondary (`#18ded0` — Aether Radium / Eldritch Cyan):** The unearthly ionization of vacuum tube filaments, cyclopean glyphs, telemetric readings, and critical alert states. Used sparingly to preserve maximum visual impact against dense abyssal backdrops.
- **Tertiary (`#2a1538` — Void Plum / Abyssal Miasma):** The shadowed connective tissue of the universe. Applied to sunken panels, internal component housings, depth-fill overlays, and esoteric container backings.
- **Neutral (`#080a0c` — Void Abyssal Black):** Deep space matter, oxidized carbon, and light-devouring obsidian surfaces. Forms the root canvas over which mechanical apparatuses are constructed.

### Supporting Chromatics & Material Tones
- **Verdigris (`#2c5a52`):** Used for micro-patina borders, oxidized weld lines, and inactive physical channels.
- **Antique Parchment (`#eae3d2`):** The primary typographic ink on dark fields; mimics preserved velum and aged ivory measurement scales.
- **Gauge Amber (`#f59e0b`):** Warning pressure thresholds, incandescent pilot lights, and vacuum-tube filament warmth.
- **Wrought Iron & Slate (`#161a1f`, `#21262d`):** Machined chassis framing, divider grooves, and baseline inset card surfaces.

## Typography

The typography system operates on three deliberate conceptual axes:
1. **The Scholarly Aristocracy (`Bodoni Moda`):** High-contrast transitional serifs for dramatic heraldic headlines, chapter demarcations, and forbidden tomes. Its sharp serifs evoke imperial patent engravings and chiselled monument stones.
2. **The Occult Narrative (`EB Garamond`):** Used for long-form exposition, archival correspondence, and lore descriptions. Warm, bookish, and human, providing breathing room against stark metallic structures.
3. **The Telegraphic Telemetry (`Space Mono`):** Monospaced, razor-sharp technical figures for atmospheric gauges, chronometer readouts, machine serial designations, and interactive controls. All label caps apply expanded tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06em - 0.1em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a disciplined 12-column architectural grid inspired by draughtsman blueprints and mechanical patent folios. Spacing is rhythmic, relying on multiples of 4px/8px to mimic interlocking gear ratios and precision lathe turns.

### Breakpoints & Fluidity
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 48px outer canvas margin, 24px column gutters. Complex instruments can span multi-tier arrays (e.g., 4-column telemetry monitor flanked by 8-column historical archives).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px margins, 16px gutters. Secondary dials collapse into drawer panels; headers stack hierarchically.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Ornamental outer chassis borders streamline down to minimal double-fillet strokes to maximize internal reading apertures.

### Compositional Rhythms
Sections are framed within structural brass boundaries. Canvas gaps do not leave empty void drifts; instead, negative space is anchored with faint Calabi-Yau geometry watermarks, etched coordinate guides, or subtle vacuum-tube illumination rings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use generic grey drop-shadows. Depth is built via physical layering of materials, brass bezels, inset stamps, and aetheric glow radiance.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Recessed / Engraved (Level -1):**
   - Inner stamped chassis, data wells, vacuum chambers.
   - Built using deep inset shadows: `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9), inset 0 0 12px rgba(8, 10, 12, 0.95);`
   - Border: 1px solid `#1a2027` with a top-edge highlight of 1px `#2a323d`.

2. **Chassis Bedplate (Level 0):**
   - The primary background stratum (`#080a0c`).
   - Matte, low-reflectance charcoal iron texture. Contains subtle crosshatch schematic watermarks at 4% opacity.

3. **Machined Plates & Cards (Level 1):**
   - Surface color: `#0d1014` layered over `#2a1538` tinting.
   - Border: Dual-line boundary (1px solid `#c49346` at 30% opacity, 2px interior gap, 1px solid `#c49346` at 60% opacity).
   - Cast Shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(196, 147, 70, 0.15)`.

4. **Raised Dials & Floating Instruments (Level 2):**
   - Bezels, manometers, floating toolbars.
   - Outer mechanical bevels with directional brass gradient strokes (`linear-gradient(135deg, #ffd276 0%, #a77b38 50%, #4a3210 100%)`).
   - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.95), 0 0 20px rgba(24, 222, 208, 0.12)`.

5. **Aetheric Emission / Overdrive (Level 3):**
   - Modal containers, eldritch artifacts, active containment grids.
   - Glow halos: Layered cyan and amber bloom (`0 0 25px rgba(24, 222, 208, 0.35), 0 0 60px rgba(24, 222, 208, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework is uncompromisingly **Sharp (`0`)**, echoing industrial boiler plating, cold-cut sheet brass, precision bevelled optical glass, and ancient megalithic masonry.

- **Chamfered & Bevelled Corners:** While corner radiuses remain 0px, major cards and trigger buttons utilize 45-degree corner notches (chamfers) cut at 6px to 10px via CSS `clip-path: polygon(...)`.
- **Rivet Studs:** Inset fasteners appear at cardinal card vertices, rendered as 4px circular studs (`#c49346` with inner metallic shadow and directional highlight).
- **Concentric Circles:** Gauges, dials, and occult containment rings utilize pure mathematical circles, contrasting against strictly rectilinear framing.

## Components

### Buttons & Actuators
- **Primary Kinetic Switch:** Rectangular with clipped 6px diagonal chamfers. Background is deep iron-black with a metallic gradient stroke (`#ffd276` to `#a77b38`). Label set in `label-lg` uppercase brass text. Hover initiates a warm amber back-glow (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`) and a 1px depressed downward displacement (`transform: translateY(1px)`).
- **Aether Discharge Button (Destructive / Apex Action):** Inset with eldritch cyan inner-glow. On hover, the border pulses with an oscillating vacuum ionization effect (`#18ded0`), and text shifts from ivory to radiant cyan.
- **Rocker / Toggle Switches:** Steampunk physical toggles flanked by stamped brass status markings (`OFF / ENGAGED`) in `label-sm`.

### Input Fields & Telemetry Readouts
- **Aperture Input Field:** Sunken surface (`Level -1`) with a faint verdigris-tinted inner glow (`#2c5a52`). Cursor is an illuminated cyan block (`#18ded0`). Typing emits a subtle filament flicker.
- **Vacuum Gauge Readouts:** Circular dial indicators encased in a knurled brass bezel with a domed glass highlight overlay. Needle rendered in copper wire, pivoting over a parchment scale inscribed with Roman numerals and logarithmic intervals.

### Cards & Esoteric Dossiers
- **Riveted Plate Dossier:** Multi-layer card wrapped in a 2px dual-rule border. Corner joints feature exposed brass rivet heads. Header bar is separated by an etched horizontal rule with a center-aligned occult sigil or Calabi-Yau geometry badge.
- **Specimen Containment Card:** Features a deep plum-black backdrop (`#120d18`) overlaid with faint biomechanical tentacle cross-hatching etched directly into the background texture.

### Chips, Badges & Seals
- **Wax & Brass Stamp Badges:** Strict rectangular tags with stamped borders, displaying classification tiers (`TOP SECRET // ESOTERIC CLASS IV`).
- **Telemetry Indicators:** Miniature glowing vacuum filament lamps displaying system power, pressure, or planar alignment via three states: Extinguished (dark grey `#21262d`), Idle (amber `#f59e0b`), and Supercritical (cyan `#18ded0`).

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- **Mechanical Relays:** Checkboxes are square, rivet-rimmed boxes. Checked state displays a glowing brass saltire cross (`╳`) or an alchemical transmutation glyph in high-voltage cyan.
- **Rotary Radio Selectors:** Stepped selector knobs with knurled grips that rotate visually toward the selected label index.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Schematic Index Lists:** Rows divided by hairline brass rules (`rgba(196, 147, 70, 0.25)`). Hovering over a line item illuminates an aetheric horizontal rule that sweeps outward from the center point, accompanied by the display of precise coordinate telemetry in the right gutter.
