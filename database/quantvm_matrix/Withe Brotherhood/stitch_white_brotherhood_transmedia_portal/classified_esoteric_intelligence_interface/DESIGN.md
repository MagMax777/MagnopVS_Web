## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an esoteric occult terminal: an interface at the intersection of classified military black-budget operations, divine mechanical apparatuses, and cosmic horror containment protocols. The aesthetic evokes a sensation of accessing forbidden, compartmentalized transmedia lore through a sanctified, diegetic tactical console.

Key style pillars:
- **Military-Occult High-Tech:** Surgical, high-density data matrices, structural framing reticles, coordinate readouts, redacted intelligence blocks, and sacred geometry vector schemas.
- **Divine Machinery & Cosmic Gothic:** Sacred architectural framing blended with aerospace telemetry, brushed abyssal alloy, ritual containment rings, and cold cathode ionization.
- **Diegetic Immersion:** The interface acts as a living physical artifact inside the game universe—subtle spectral aberrations, volumetric optical blooms, low-frequency atmospheric luminescence, and pristine monolithic precision.

## Colors
The system operates exclusively within an abyssal luminance range, relying on cold light emission over absolute dark voids.

### Foundation & Depths
- `abyss-void`: `#050507` (Deep space viewport, viewport substrate)
- `abyss-base`: `#0B0D12` (Primary canvas, terminal background)
- `abyss-surface`: `#11131A` (Container tiles, containment cells, structural modules)
- `abyss-elevated`: `#191C26` (Floating panels, flyouts, contextual strata)

### Core Telemetry & Typography
- `bone-light`: `#EDEDED` (Primary inscriptions, dominant display headers, primary glyphs)
- `bone-muted`: `#C9D0D8` (Secondary intelligence text, field labels, metadata)
- `bone-faded`: `#68707D` (Non-critical telemetry, grid lines, structural frames)

### Luminescent Energy Accents
- `primary` (`#00E5FF`): Ionizing Cyan. Signifies active data streams, containment status nominal, tactical focal vectors.
- `secondary` (`#7B5CFF`): Spectral Violet. Denotes psionic resonance, anomalous entities, esoteric intelligence clearances.
- `tertiary` (`#F4C95D`): Alchemical Gold. Reserved for divine relics, celestial machinery, architectural sanctum layers.
- `anomaly-magenta` (`#D946EF`): Singularity events, corrupted telemetry, reality-distortion flags.
- `occult-emerald` (`#00FFB3`): Bio-sacred diagnostics, life-support telemetry, stabilized relic containment.
- `alert-crimson` (`#FF304F`): Direct containment breaches, terminal redactions, fatal protocol alerts.

## Typography
Typographic construction creates a severe functional hierarchy:

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Geometric, precise, and authoritative. Display titles should strictly use uppercase casing with generous tracking to emulate etched monument plates and defense telemetry consoles.
- **Narrative & Records (Manrope):** Balanced, modern, and neutral. Used for dense archival logs, field documentation, and classified dossier descriptions.
- **Telemetry & Classified Readouts (JetBrains Mono):** Dense, monospaced metadata, timestamp tracks, coordinates, clearances, and cryptic protocol identifiers. Rendered in all-caps with extended tracking.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a modular 12-column tactical HUD grid with precision framing.

- **Grid Alignment:** Surfaces adhere strictly to 8px base micro-rhythms. Layout components anchor to mechanical grid brackets and HUD crosshairs.
- **Form Factors:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** Full multi-pane tactical split (Left: Index Dossier / Center: Volumetric Relic Stage / Right: Real-time Esoteric Readout). 12 columns, 1.5rem gutter, 3rem margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Relic viewport switches to a stacked vertical viewport; side-telemetry condenses into collapsible instrument drawers.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-stream containment log. Tactical reticles compress to header/footer edge indicators; margins tighten to 1rem.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is conveyed through optical ionization, surface layering, and planar containment borders rather than conventional diffuse drop shadows.

- **Base Canvas:** Deepest layer (`#050507`), overlaid with subtle scanline vectors and faint polar coordinate watermarks at 4% opacity.
- **Primary Surfaces:** Matte dark titanium (`#0B0D12` to `#11131A`) surfaced with crisp 1px borders (`#191C26` or `#00E5FF` at 15% opacity).
- **Volumetric Glow & Field Luminescence:**
  - Interactive elements and alert boundaries emit narrow spectral backlights: `0 0 12px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.25)` or `0 0 20px rgba(123, 92, 255, 0.20)`.
  - Breach/Critical states utilize alert crimson aura: `0 0 16px rgba(255, 48, 79, 0.35)`.
- **Classified Redaction Plates:** Completely opaque black bars (`#000000`) bordered by micro-hatched warning striping at 10% opacity.

## Shapes
Sharp angular architecture (`roundedness: 0`). Curved primitives are strictly forbidden; corners are sharp 90-degree joints or 45-degree chamfered tactical bevels (`clip-path: polygon`).

- **Containment Brackets:** Corner reticles (`L`-shaped markers) frame all cards, modals, and viewport boundaries.
- **Dividers:** 1px hairline horizontal and vertical conduits with terminal crosshairs (`+`) at intersections.
- **Badges & Node Indicators:** Hexagonal, diamond, or pure rectangular geometries.

## Components

### Action Triggers (Buttons)
- **Primary Operational:** High-energy ionizing cyan stroke (1px), deep abyss background (`#0B0D12`), text in `label-md` uppercase (`#00E5FF`). Hover triggers a cyan flood fill with black ink typography (`#050507`) and an inner volumetric pulse (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)`). Corners feature 45-degree chamfer cuts.
- **Secondary Arcane:** Bordered in `#7B5CFF` (30% opacity) with spectral violet typography. Hover increases border opacity to 100% with faint violet surface diffusion.
- **Critical Abort:** Crimson border (`#FF304F`), glowing strobe on hover, micro-coded security key icon prefix.

### Status Indicators & Badges (Chips)
- Sharp rectangular capsules framed by 1px borders. Text rendered in `label-sm` with a preceding glowing telemetry dot:
  - Nominal: Emerald (`#00FFB3`)
  - Esoteric/Psionic: Spectral Violet (`#7B5CFF`)
  - Containment Strained: Alchemical Gold (`#F4C95D`)
  - Breach Active: Crimson (`#FF304F`) with subtle 1Hz pulse animation.

### Data Dossiers (Cards)
- Composed of `#11131A` flat background, 1px `#191C26` boundary, and active corner registration marks (`+` or `L` indices). 
- Header bar includes monospaced clearance tags (e.g., `// SEC-LVL: COSMIC-V`) separated by a hairline rule from the body.

### Intelligence Fields & Data Inputs
- Inverted dark bays (`#050507`) with 1px inset borders. Text entry uses `JetBrains Mono` in `#EDEDED`. 
- Focus state shifts bottom accent line to `#00E5FF` with a monospaced terminal cursor block (`█`).

### Toggles, Checkboxes & Verification Nodes
- Square, razor-edged boxes (16x16px). Unchecked: `#191C26` border. Checked: Filled with `#00E5FF` enclosing a centered black diamond sigil.

### Classified Special Components
- **Redaction Block:** Solid black container with `[CLASSIFIED]` or `[DATA PURGED]` watermark exposed only upon verified clearance hover.
- **Sigil Frame:** Circular and nonagonal vector schemas inscribed with geometric lines, slowly rotating at 0.05 RPM as background data visualizers.
- **Telemetry HUD Compass:** Fixed cardinal indicator showing coordinate markers and real-time anomaly resonance frequencies.
